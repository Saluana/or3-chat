# Completion guidance

State the result and validation concisely, using the format requested by the user.
Headings and a fixed section order are not required.

Include additional facts when they affect the user's next decision:

- Runtime, architecture, or trust changes.
- Material grants, network access, or secret-handling changes.
- Artifact path/digest and actual installation or activation status.
- Recovery, disable, uninstall, or rollback steps for consequential changes.
- Unresolved material risks or failed/unavailable checks.

Do not claim completion from scaffolding alone, infer activation from packaging, or promote an artifact after known failed validation.
Fixture cases describe task-specific evidence, not mandatory report headings.
